## Start a task

Tasks assigned to you appear in **My tasks**, grouped by project and updating live.

**Start** assigns the task to you, moves it to *In progress*, and creates a branch such as `username/key-7-task-title`. When you open a pull request from that branch, bld.t links it to the task.

The task for your current branch is shown in the status bar. Click it to move it to review or done.
